## Findings and applied changes

The first game's clear columns against nearly black recesses make entrances readable with few colors. The sequel combines ornamental horizontal bands, lattice windows, carved portals and layered rocky silhouettes. Sampled action frames provide pose references but do not justify claims about exact timings or fully analyzed motion.

The game's harbor now has an original raised terrace with lattice arches, burgundy and gold ornament, carved columns and a turquoise mosaic. Its caves have original recessed stone entrances, highlighted columns, segmented arch ornaments and dark interior masonry. Existing environmental layers retain their parallax. The combat plane remains unobstructed. No downloaded game sprites were integrated.
